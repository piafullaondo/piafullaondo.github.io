About Pia Fullaondo

In 2020, as the global pandemic was coming to an end, I began studying a joint Master of Arts degree in Pure Mathematics and Philosophy. At the time, I often questioned the practicality of focusing on these subjects in their purest forms, but it turned out to be one of the best decisions I’ve made.

During my studies, I focused on statistics and data visualisation on the mathematics side, and on artificial agency and its relationship to moral agency in philosophy. Although I’ve since moved out of academia, these questions still shape how I think - if anything, even more so now from an industry perspective.

I currently work as a Lead Data Scientist in the FinTech sector, where I see and shape first-hand how technology impacts users day to day and how businesses respond to that. I’m particularly interested in how this relationship continues to evolve, and in playing a part in shaping how technology is integrated into society.
