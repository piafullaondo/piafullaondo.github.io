Project

Real face or AI-generated fake?

Quiz app + article on spotting synthetic faces

What it does

A short web quiz that shows a mix of real photographs and GAN-generated faces, side by side, and asks the visitor to guess which is which. Scores are tallied at the end alongside the telltale artefacts (odd earrings, blurred backgrounds, asymmetric glasses) that give synthetic faces away.
